## Suggested outline

1. **Service** - what the app does and who operates it.
2. **Eligibility and acceptable use** - age, credentials, prohibited use
   (abuse, scraping, bypassing limits).
3. **Subscriptions and advertising** - Google Play billing, auto-renewal,
   refunds, Pro = ad-free, ads for free users, no ads on trust-sensitive
   screens.
4. **Third-party services** - Supabase, Google, RevenueCat, AdMob, Play.
5. **Intellectual property** - app branding vs user content.
6. **Termination and disclaimers** - "as is", liability limits, AI/derived
   content caveats when applicable.
7. **Changes** - current version always at `https://<slug>.mogate.tech/terms`.
8. **Contact** - support email.
